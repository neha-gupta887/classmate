What is CampusMate?+

CampusMate is an independently operated service-provider website for Chandigarh University students. You can browse published services or send a custom enquiry and discuss your needs directly on WhatsApp.

Is CampusMate officially affiliated with CU?+

No. CampusMate is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of Chandigarh University. References to the university identify the student community this platform is intended for.

How do I request a service?+

Open a published service and select the WhatsApp enquiry button, or fill in the custom enquiry form. Review the prepared message in WhatsApp and tap Send. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking; acceptance, price, and delivery must be agreed in the conversation.

What if the service I need isn't listed?+

Use the custom enquiry form to describe your requirement, preferred deadline, and optional budget. Availability and feasibility will be discussed on WhatsApp. A submitted enquiry does not guarantee the service can be provided.

How are pricing, payment, and updates handled?+

Final scope, pricing, payment arrangements, delivery, and any cancellation or refund terms must be confirmed in WhatsApp before you commit. This website does not collect payments or provide an account dashboard. Follow up in the same chat for updates.

Can I send documents or supporting files?+

You can share relevant files directly in WhatsApp when needed. This website does not upload or store documents. Avoid sending passwords, identity documents, or unrelated private information. Academic enquiries must comply with your university's academic-integrity rules.
