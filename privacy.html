THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This notice describes the current public CampusMate website and its WhatsApp enquiry process. CampusMate is an independent student service provider and is not affiliated with Chandigarh University.

Information entered on this website

The custom enquiry form collects a name, category, title, description, and optional deadline and budget to prepare a WhatsApp message. These form values are processed in your browser. This website does not submit them to its own database or save them to localStorage. They may remain in the page until you clear it, reload it, or close your browser; your browser may independently preserve form values.

When you continue to WhatsApp

Selecting the WhatsApp button opens a link containing your prepared message. The message text is shared with WhatsApp as part of that link and may appear in your browser history. Review it and tap Send in WhatsApp to deliver it to the CampusMate contact at +91 89577 46102.

Once sent, the recipient can see your message, the WhatsApp account information visible to them, and any attachments you choose to share. Enquiry information is used to discuss requirements, availability, quotes, delivery, and follow-up. WhatsApp processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. This website cannot delete messages from WhatsApp or control its retention practices.

Supporting documents and sensitive information

This website does not upload or store files. If you choose to share a document in WhatsApp, share only what is relevant and redact other people's information where possible. Do not send passwords, payment authentication codes, university account credentials, or unnecessary identity documents.

Website infrastructure and external resources

The hosting provider may process technical access logs, including IP address, browser information, and requested pages, to deliver and secure the website. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a photograph from Unsplash; those providers receive the network information necessary to respond to your browser's requests. No advertising trackers or analytics scripts have been added to this website by CampusMate.

Public catalog and private enquiries

Service listings are public. Student enquiries are not published on this website. There are no student accounts, private dashboards, or website-based messaging records in this version.

Your choices and questions

You may browse without submitting an enquiry. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent to the CampusMate contact, or to ask about retention before sharing information, message +91 89577 46102 on WhatsApp. Some records may need to be retained where required by applicable law. The operator will need to verify that the request relates to you.

Changes to this notice

This notice should be updated before adding account registration, online payments, analytics, database-based enquiries, or other data-processing features.
